### Objective:
Why are employees leaving? Create an interactive dashboard that allows users to filter by Region, Age Group, Department.

### Dataset: HR Employee Attrition (1470 employees)

### Tools Used:
- Power BI / Tableau
- Excel for data prep

### Dashboard Features:
- Filters: Region | Age Group | Department
- KPI Cards: Total Employees 1470, Attrition 16.1%, Avg Age 36
- Charts: Department wise Attrition Bar, Age Group Donut, Salary Slab Pie

### Key Insights:
1. Sales Dept highest attrition - 20%
2. Young employees 25-34 age group - 41% attrition
3. Low Salary employees - 48% attrition - Main reason for leaving
4. Recommendation: Increase salary for 25-34 age group in Sales
